# AI Tool Disclosure

## Tool Used

- **Kimi K3** — large language model

## Scope of AI Assistance

AI assistance was used **only** for:

1. **Statistical methodology consultation**: discussion of the 72-hour landmark design for
   guarantee-time bias, the E-value and tipping-point approach to unmeasured confounding,
   inverse-probability weighting for measurement intensity, regression-dilution correction,
   restricted mean survival time, and Fine-Gray competing-risks modelling.

2. **Study-design consultation**: discussion of the structural confounding between baseline
   troponin concentration and dynamic change, and of classification-uncertainty bias in
   group-then-survival analyses of biomarker trajectories.

3. **Code assistance**: drafting and debugging of the analysis scripts and of the figure-
   generation code that calls standard R (survival, cmprsk, rms) and Python (matplotlib)
   routines.

## What AI Did NOT Do

- AI did **not** access, process, or analyse any research data
- AI did **not** write or draft any part of the manuscript text
- AI did **not** interpret statistical results or draw clinical conclusions
- AI did **not** make scientific or clinical judgments
- AI did **not** contribute to the study concept, hypothesis, or clinical reasoning

## Author Oversight

All AI-assisted outputs were critically reviewed by the research team. Every analytical
decision was taken by the authors on the basis of their own expertise, the statistical
analysis plan, and the published literature. The authors take full responsibility for the
study design, the statistical analysis, and the reporting of results.

## Compliance

This disclosure follows:

- **ICMJE** recommendations on artificial intelligence in scientific authorship
- **Nature Portfolio** policy on AI-assisted research
- **STROBE** reporting guidelines for observational studies
- **COPE** guidelines on AI use in publications

## Transparency Statement

The authors believe in full transparency regarding the use of AI tools in research. This
disclosure is provided so that readers and reviewers can assess the methodological
integrity of the study. The core scientific contributions — study conceptualisation,
clinical interpretation, analytical decision-making, and manuscript writing — were
performed exclusively by the human authors.
